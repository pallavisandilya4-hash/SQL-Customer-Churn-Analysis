# SQL-Based Customer Retention & Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing telecom customer data using MySQL to identify customer churn patterns, customer retention trends, revenue contribution, and customer segmentation.

The main objective of this project was to extract meaningful business insights from raw customer data using SQL queries and analytical techniques. The analysis helps understand customer behavior, identify high-risk churn customers, recognize high-value customers, and support data-driven business decisions.

This project demonstrates the practical implementation of SQL concepts in solving real-world business problems related to customer retention and revenue analysis.

---

# Business Problem Statement

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for telecom companies because losing customers directly impacts revenue and business growth.

The company wanted to analyze:

- Which customers are leaving the company
- Which customer groups generate the highest revenue
- Which payment methods and contract types show higher churn
- Which customers are loyal and long-term
- How customer segmentation can support business strategies

---

# Objectives

- Analyze customer churn behavior
- Identify high-value customers
- Understand revenue contribution
- Perform customer segmentation
- Identify customer retention trends
- Extract actionable business insights using SQL

# Project Workflow

## Phase 1: Data Exploration & Basic SQL Analysis

In this phase, customer data was explored and analyzed using basic SQL queries.

### Analysis Performed

- Total Customers Analysis
- Total Revenue Analysis
- Average Revenue Analysis
- Churn Rate Analysis
- Contract Type Analysis
- Revenue by Internet Service
- Customer Segmentation

### Key Insights

- Month-to-month customers showed the highest churn.
- High-value customers contributed significantly to total revenue.
- Long-term customers showed stronger retention and loyalty.

---

## Phase 2: Joins & Advanced SQL Analysis

In this phase, advanced SQL concepts such as joins and business relationship analysis were performed.

### Analysis Performed

- Payment Method Analysis
- Revenue by Payment Method
- Churn by Payment Method
- Customer & Payment Data Join Analysis

### Key Insights

- Electronic check users had the highest churn count.
- Payment behavior showed strong relationships with customer retention.

---

## Phase 3: Subqueries & Customer Insights

Advanced SQL subqueries were used to identify important customer groups.

### Analysis Performed

- Above Average Revenue Customers
- Long-Term Loyal Customers
- High Revenue Customer Identification

### Key Insights

- Customers generating above-average revenue were identified as high-value customers.
- Long-term customers contributed high revenue and business stability.

---

# Key Business Insights

1. Month-to-month contract customers showed the highest churn rate.

2. Electronic check users experienced higher churn compared to other payment methods.

3. Long-term customers contributed significantly to revenue and retention.

4. High-value customers represented an important customer segment for business growth.

5. Customer segmentation helped classify customers into Low, Medium, and High Value groups for better business understanding.
